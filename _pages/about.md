I'm a climate scientist studying intraseasonal rainfall variability to help inform agricultural decision-making. I'm currently a Ph.D. Candidate in the [Department of Geography](https://geog.ufl.edu/) at the University of Florida, working with [Berry Wen](https://www.stonybrook.edu/somas/people/faculty/berry-wen.html) (now at Stony Brook University).

My work is motivated by a simple question: _What matters most for livelihoods, the total amount of rain a season brings or when, how often, and how intensely it falls_? I've [found](https://doi.org/10.1175/JHM-D-25-0080.1) that examining seasonal rainfall totals together with rainfall timing, frequency, and intensity gives a fuller picture of how rainy seasons are changing across East Africa. I've also shown that understanding how the rainy season onset relates to other rainfall characteristics within the season can help us understand how the season is likely to unfold.

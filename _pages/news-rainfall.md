*June 24, 2026 · UCSB Climate Hazards Center*

The UCSB Climate Hazards Center featured our research on how rainy seasons are changing across eastern Africa, highlighting why seasonal rainfall totals alone do not tell the whole story.

Our study examines changes in rainy-season timing, duration, rainfall frequency, and intensity. A wetter season can result from more frequent rain, heavier rain, or both, with different implications for agriculture and water resources.

The feature discusses these findings and their relevance to agricultural planning and food security.
